Chain Reaction — Privacy policy

Effective 9 November 2026 · Package dev.shervey.chainreaction

Chain Reaction is an offline game. It does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

Data the app stores

All of the following stays on your device in Preferences DataStore and is removed when the app is uninstalled:

  • Display preferences: theme, colour-blind palette and reduce motion.
  • Sound and haptics toggles.
  • Local statistics: matches played, moves, win split, longest chain and explosions.
  • One saved in-progress match, cleared when the match ends or you quit.

Data the app sends

None. The app has no internet permission and no networking code. It has no accounts, ads, in-app purchases or analytics.

Permissions

The app declares a single permission, VIBRATE, used only for optional haptic feedback that you can disable in Settings.

Children

The app collects no data, so it collects no data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears at this address with an updated effective date.
